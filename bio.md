# Bio

I’m Daniele Zanaga, a physicist and data researcher based in Belgium.

I studied physics at the University of Parma and moved to Belgium for a PhD in Physics at the
University of Antwerp, where I worked on nanomaterials using electron microscopy, image
analysis, and algorithm development.

After a short period developing heart-rate monitoring applications, I joined VITO Remote Sensing.
Today I work on satellite imagery, global land-cover mapping, vegetation and biodiversity change,
AI, computer vision, and large-scale data products.

I’m interested in the space between science and making: turning complex observations into useful
tools and exploring new ideas.
