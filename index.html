I’m Daniele Zanaga, a physicist and data researcher based in Belgium.

I studied physics at the University of Parma and moved to Belgium for a PhD in Physics at the University of Antwerp, where I worked on nanomaterials using electron microscopy, image analysis, and algorithm development.

After a short period developing heart-rate monitoring applications, I joined VITO Remote Sensing. Today I work on satellite imagery, global land-cover mapping, vegetation and biodiversity change, AI, computer vision, and large-scale data products that help us understand the health of our planet.

I’m interested in the space between science and making: turning complex observations into useful tools, exploring new ideas, and building small experiments that might grow into something more. Zengarden is my sandbox for those explorations.
